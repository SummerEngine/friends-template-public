# Items and events of the show

A race of THE SHOW that carries item boxes (levels 29-32: `item_boxes` in the level file) has **items** a runner picks up and uses, and **events** the clock hands to a runner at the back.
Everything a team can change about them lives in the cast (`show.items`, below); the template's own texts are generic. The mechanics are the engine's and locked.

| id | kind | what happens | hits | template banner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `smash` | item | a dark shadow grows alone under the leader for 0.5 s, then a giant white-gloved hand (a Blender model, `tools/item_models/hand_glove.py`, about 6 m from wrist to fingertip in the game, its palm 3.6 m across) drops into it over the rest of the 1.4 s (it follows, cocks back, locks; the victim's camera looks up at it), slams, and flattens the character (Y scale 0.11, a swell of 1.15 across, frozen) for 2.5 s: a slap, a dust ring, a shock ring, cracks, a camera punch with a shake, the banner. The hand rests ON the highest surface under it (rays down at its palm and fingers; a spinner bar sweeping through lifts it). It lets go 0.55 s before the pin ends and the flat character lies there alone with stars over it, then springs back over 0.45 s with a couple of damped bounces. Nothing happens to the user; a leader already flat keeps the item with its player | the racer in the lead (never the user; the second place when the user leads) | `SMASHED!` |
| `call` | item | a homing shot (a blue ring with a handset, a comet trail) leaves the user's chest in an arc and curves onto the nearest racer ahead at 20 m/s (it dissolves after 6 s, or when the target leaves the race; with nobody ahead the item stays); a hit opens a call window on the victim's screen: an INCOMING CALL toast for 0.35 s, then a dark video-call window over 72 % of the screen by area (centred, the game stays visible round it; its content stays inside 17-83 % of the width, so the objectives and ITEM pill, the scoreboard, the timer and the hints land on the window's dark gutters) with the footage (the cast's `media`, else a spreadsheet drawn in code), the callers as letter tiles and a toolbar with a big red DECLINE button. The victim keeps running. It lasts 6 s but at least 3.5 s: every GRAB press of the victim takes 0.5 s off (the authority counts them from `host.presses`; the window shakes and squishes DECLINE on the owner's own tap count, `game_run.gd grab_taps`, and a ring shows the seconds left and the taps landed). The others see a ringing phone over the victim's head | the nearest racer ahead of the user | `INCOMING CALL` |
| `flip` | item | steering is flipped (left is right, forward is back) for 7 s; the victim's camera does one barrel roll and a CONTROLS FLIPPED pill counts the seconds | everybody but the user | `FLIPPED!` |
| `glitch` | item | the character is drawn only every 0.5 s on every client (a lagging pose: jerk, hold, jerk) while the victim's camera keeps turning smoothly; the victim's screen is a trip (a full-screen shader in the post layer: big slow swells, split colour channels, a drifting hue, double vision; camera roll ±10°, lens ±9°, a flickering PING 999 pill), eased in over 0.5 s, held, out over 0.5 s, for 5.5 s | everybody ahead of the user (the item stays with its player when nobody is) | `GLITCH!` |
| `dance` | item | a THROWN phone (it leaves the hand along the way the user faces with a little lift, a mild aim assist bends it towards a racer inside a 32° cone up to 45 m ahead, so it hits when thrown roughly right and misses when thrown wrong; it pops in confetti on the floor or after 2.5 s, the item is spent either way); a hit: for 3 s the racer stands and dances and cannot move (on every screen) and wears the plain base look (the bean head, no hat, hair or hand item: `set_look_over` of the rig, the own look comes back exactly), a REC dot and a LIVE tag float over its head for the others; on its own screen dark bars close in to a vertical 9:16 frame, the camera swings round to its face and zooms in, and the frame wears a pulsing pink-cyan filter, floating hearts, a LIVE badge, a climbing view counter, heart / comment / share buttons, a blinking REC and a few comments | the first racer other than the user it touches | `FILMING!` |
| `boost` | event | +45 % speed for 5 s, a gold glow (aura, ring, sparks, a burst of "$") and speed lines on the owner's screen, whoever it runs into is flung (2.2 times the shove, no kick back) | a racer at the back, every 20-30 s | `SPEED UP!` |
| `soar` | event | a super jump (2 times the take-off, 4 times the height) and one more jump in the air (a double jump, 0.92 of the first) for 5.5 s; the owner's screen counts views 10K, 100K, 1M at its right edge, the others read a small VIEWS pill over the runner and see its cyan-pink ribbon | a racer at the back, every 20-30 s | `SUPER JUMP!` |
| `eggplant` | experiment (its own clock) | a giant eggplant is strapped to a runner's back: 25 % slower, lower jumps, it can only be passed on by a bump or a GRAB and pops after 20 s or at the finish line ("Hot eggplant", below) | a front runner more likely, one at a time, the first at about 12 s then about every 45 s | `EGGPLANT!` |

## Flow

* **Banners.** The experiments' banners (item blue, alert red, boon gold) are small (`ShowBanner.small_wide`, about a third of the screen) and short (1.3-1.4 s hold); keep the words to a word or two.
* **Boxes.** A rounded cube with a "?" on every side, standing on a small pedestal (nothing floats; the pedestal ring wears the brand accent), 2-4 in a row, rows at two or three spots of a course. A racer without an item that
  walks through one takes a **roll**: the HUD slot spins for 1 s (a tick per icon flip), then shows the item (a bell); the box bursts into shards and is back after 8 s. Boxes are views: the authority decides from positions (a racer within 1.15 m of the box's spot, feet within -0.6..2 m of it).
* **Roll weights** follow the race position (`weights` in `modes/show/items/items.json`: leader, middle, last, blended along the field): the leader never rolls `smash`, `call` or `glitch` (nobody to hit), back runners get the strong ones.
* **Using it.** One item at a time. ITEM: **E** on the keyboard (**G** and **B** for two players on one keyboard), **Y** or the left bumper on a pad, the round ITEM button on a phone (it shows the item's icon, over GRAB). The press counter rides in the input as the extra key
  `item_seq` (`network/input_wire.gd` carries extra keys; `network/game.gd valid_input` checks it); the authority acts on the change. A press while the roll spins, or with nothing held, is spent.
* **Events.** `event.first` 40-55 s into the race, then every 50-70 s, when the show's event gate lets it (one interrupting event at a time, 15 s apart: `docs/party-show.md` "One interrupting event at a time"; the held items below are NOT gated, they are a player's own choice): the clock picks a racer, back runners likelier (weight `(0.15 + place/(n-1))^2`), `boost` or `soar` (never the same twice running).
* **HUD.** Top left under the objective: the **item pill** (show look: dark pill, the item's icon as a plain glyph straight on the pill with no disc behind it (while the box rolls the icons flip through with no ring either), the name in slanted words, the key). The CONTROLS FLIPPED and PING 999 pills have the item's icon at their left end and a red outline. Banners use the show's banner (`style/show_banner.gd` kinds `item` blue, `alert` red, `boon` lime); a line about another player's item shows at the top centre.
* A racer who crosses the goal drops its item and every effect; the round's end clears everything.

## Who does what (networking)

* The **authority** (`modes/show/items/items.gd`, one script per item `<id>_rules.gd`, extends `item_rule.gd`) owns the boxes, who holds what, the effects `fx[]` (`{id, slot, kind, dur, age, a, b, src}`, the `active_effects` of the slots) and the projectiles. It sends them as `items` in the show's state
  (`held`, `spin`, `boxes` bitmask, `fx`, `proj`, `ev`), a few hundred bytes. It never moves a courier except with `host.send_to`.
* **Couriers move on their own machines** and the authority only checks poses, so an effect that changes how a courier moves is applied by the owner's machine from the replicated effect: the item's view (`<id>_view.gd`, extends `item_look.gd`) returns `mods(fx)`;
  `items_view.gd` merges them into `game_run.gd item_mods[slot]` (`freeze`, `invert`, `speed`, `jump`, `air_jumps` (extra jumps in the air), `dash`, `no_flop`: `courier_motion.gd mods`; `invert` flips the steering in `game_run.gd item_move`), `item_power` (the shove multiplier, `players/player_contact.gd`),
  `rig_lag` (a rig drawn once per period), `rig_scales` (a squash) and the local camera's `fx` (`follow_camera.gd`: roll, fov, zoom).
* **Clients render the local effects**: the wobble shader, the letterbox, the call window, the stutter, the hand, the projectiles; nothing a view draws decides anything.
* Local play (one machine) runs the same rules and views in one process: `host.presses` reads the local devices.

## Alone

A show for one player has one racer, so nobody can be hit: the boxes (levels 29-32) are there all the same and hand out the events' effects as items, a **boost** or a **soar** (the pill shows it, one press of ITEM uses it on yourself, its banner is the effect's); the clock hands out nothing. The attack items exist only in the lab, where they hit the user itself (`items.self_test`): the hand comes down on you, FLIP flips your controls, GLITCH melts your screen, CALL rings you, DANCE films you. The boxes, the pill and the lab work in a single round picked in the lobby, in THE SHOW, and with 2+ players (`tools/modes/items_flow_check.gd` plays the real flow for all three).

## The manager and the item keys

A round that has THE MANAGER (`docs/party-show.md` "The manager (experiment)") reads ITEM (E, Y, the ITEM button) and the number keys 1-3 as the manager's pick while its prompt is open (and for 0.6 s after): `items.gd _manager_asks` leaves those presses alone, so the manager's pick uses no item and the lab hands out nothing.

## Item lab (testing)

`show.item_lab: true` in the cast (on in the Summer build) turns the lab on in races that have item boxes: **1-7** on the keyboard (also on one keyboard for two players: the first player's) put an item straight into your slot (1 smash, 2 call, 3 flip, 4 glitch, 5 dance) or make the event happen to you now (6 boost, 7 soar), replacing what you hold; on a phone the ITEM button is always there and a long press (0.5 s) cycles through the seven. A small pill under the item pill reads `1-7: TEST ITEMS`.
The press rides in the input as the extra key `test_seq` (`seq * 8 + number`, `network/game.gd valid_input`); the authority ignores it when the lab is off. With the lab on the attack items always find somebody: `smash` hits the leader other than the user (the next best when the user leads), `call` homes on the nearest racer ahead, or behind when in the lead, `glitch` blurs the racers ahead, or the pack behind when in the lead, `dance` is thrown at anyone.

## The cast (`show.items`)

```json
"show": {"items": [
  {"id": "smash", "name": "BIG HAND", "banner": "A HAND FROM ABOVE", "line": "{user} SENT A HAND DOWN ON {victim}",
   "voice": "items/smash.ogg", "sfx": "items/smash_hit.ogg", "tuning": {"pin": 2.5}},
  {"id": "call", "media": "items/call.ogv"},
  {"id": "glitch", "off": true}
]}
```

| Key | Limit | Does |
|---|---|---|
| `id` | `smash` `call` `flip` `glitch` `dance` `boost` `soar` `eggplant` | which item or event the entry changes; an id with no entry keeps the template's texts |
| `name` | up to 16, capitals | the item pill's text |
| `banner` | up to 30 (it is one line that shrinks to fit: shorter reads bigger), capitals | the big words on the victim's screen (the item's announcement: the primary design; a voice is only added to it) |
| `line` | up to 48 | what the others read; `{user}` and `{victim}` are filled with the names |
| `pass` | up to 48, `eggplant` only | what everybody reads when the eggplant is passed on (`{user}` passed it, `{victim}` has it now) |
| `pop` | up to 30, `eggplant` only, capitals | the big words on the carrier's screen when the eggplant pops (time up or the finish line) |
| `voice` | `items/<file>.ogg`, Ogg Vorbis mono up to 4 s and 300 KB | **the voice slot**: played on the victim's machine when the item hits; empty by default (no voice is shipped) |
| `sfx` | `items/<file>.ogg`, up to 4 s and 300 KB | the item's hit sound instead of the template's |
| `media` | `items/<file>.ogv` (Ogg Theora, under 16 MB) | `call` only: the footage in the call window instead of the generic one |
| `tuning` | numbers within the ranges of `items.json` (others are clamped) | durations and strengths (`smash` `warn` `pin` `squash`; `call` `window` `least` `tap` `speed` `cover`; `flip` `duration`; `glitch` `duration` `period` `sway`; `dance` `duration` `speed` `assist`; `boost` `duration` `speed` `shove`; `soar` `duration` `jump` `air_jumps`; `eggplant` `duration` `speed` `jump` `first` `every` `immune` `reach` `bias`) |
| `off` | `true` | the item is not in this show (never rolled, never handed out) |

Files live in `demo/cast/items/`. Missing files fall back to the template; `tools/cast_check.py` checks every key and file.

## Hot eggplant (experiment)

EXPERIMENT, switchable: `demo/modes/show/items/hot_eggplant.json` (`format: hot-eggplant-1`, a top-level `on` and `levels."29".."32"."on"`, the style of `levels/edge_bumpers.json`); delete the file or set `"on": false` and the races run without it. The rule is `items/eggplant_rules.gd`, the look `items_ui/eggplant_view.gd` and `eggplant_mesh.gd`, the numbers and the template's texts `items.json` `"eggplant"`, the cast's words `show.items[]` with `id` `eggplant` (`name`, `banner`, `line`, `pass`, `pop`, `tuning`, `off`). It is the eighth kind of the catalog (`Catalog.KINDS`, index 7: the wire's order), in neither `HELD` (no box rolls it) nor `EVENTS` (the clock's boost / soar draw does not hand it out): it has a clock of its own.

* **The clock.** `first` s (40, plus up to 4) into the race, then about every `every` s (90, give or take a tenth) after the last handout, never two at once (the next waits for the one in play), and only when the event gate grants it (it asks again every tick; it holds the gate from the handout to the pop, `docs/party-show.md` "One interrupting event at a time"). A random racer, the leader `bias` times (3) as likely as one in the back third (the weight falls in a line from `bias` for the leader to 1 at two thirds of the way down the field, and stays 1 behind that). With two or more players there must be at least two racers still running; alone (a show for one player) it is put on the player all the same, so the penalty can be seen: nobody to pass it to, it pops in time or at the goal.
* **The penalty.** An effect `eggplant` on the carrier's slot (`a` = speed 0.75, `b` = jump 0.8, the replicated numbers): the owner's machine walks and dashes 25 % slower and jumps 0.8 times as fast off the ground (about 0.64 times as high), `item_mods` (`speed`, `dash`, `jump`) like every other effect. It cannot be dropped.
* **Passing.** The authority passes it when the carrier is within `reach` m (1.05) of another racer on about the same level (a bump: the contact is symmetric, whoever walks into the carrier gets it, whoever the carrier walks into gets it; the nearest wins) or on the rising edge of a GRAB between the two (either one grabbed the other: the grab reaches 1.25 m). A new carrier keeps it at least 0.6 s, and the racer who passed it cannot be handed it again for `immune` s (2). A pass ends the carrier's effect entry and starts one on the receiver with `src` = the passer and the time that was left, so the one 20 s clock runs through the whole chain: the state is the same few bytes.
* **The end.** After `duration` s (20) it pops where it is, and it pops when its carrier crosses the goal (or leaves the race): an END event (`items.gd Event.END`, `b` = the carrier). The round's end takes it off everybody without a pop event.
* **The look** (all code, no assets: `eggplant_mesh.gd`): a 3 m eggplant, a glossy purple lathe round a bent spine (the bulb 1.24 m across), a green calyx of five drooping sepals and a curled stem, a worried face on the side that looks at the camera behind the runner (eyes, brows, a mouth, a sweat drop), two orange bands round it and a belt with two shoulder straps in the runner's own rig space; it hangs on the carrier's rig, leaning back and to the left, on every machine. The first strap-on falls from the sky and lands with a squash, a pass flies the same eggplant over in a 0.55 s arc (a tumble, a landing squash), in the last 5 s it glows red, pulses faster and shakes and a number counts over the stem, the carrier's screen has the pill `<name> <seconds>` (red in the last 5 s), the pop is a flash, 120 pieces of confetti and 12 chunks of eggplant. Banners: the receiver reads `banner` (red, `EGGPLANT!`), the others `line` on the first handout and `pass` on every pass, the carrier `pop` (`DELIVERED!`) when it goes.
* **Bots.** The physics bots of `tools/modes/show_bot_check.gd` that carry it run at the nearest other racer within 9 m (a bump passes it on); alone the bot just carries it (the check asserts the handout and the pop, and with one bot that nothing was passed).
* **Checks.** `tools/modes/items_harness.gd --scenario=eggplant --show-level=31` (authority and two replicas: the clock and its bias, the penalty and its mods, the model on both machines, a bump pass with the time carried over, the immunity, a GRAB pass, the pop on time and at the finish line, the round's end); `show_bot_check.gd` (`--solo`, and with two bots) prints `SHOW_BOT eggplant {...}` and asserts a handout in every race.

## Writing an item (for a new id or a new look)

1. `demo/modes/show/items/<id>_rules.gd` extends `item_rule.gd`: `use(user) -> bool` for a held item (a rule that sends a shot at somebody sets `aimed` to that slot, so the USE line names the victim before the shot lands), or `happen(slot)` for an event, `tick(dt)`, `ended(fx)`. Use `items.tune(id, key)`, `items.add_fx`, `items.spawn`, `items.racers()`, `items.rank(slot)`, `items.ahead_of(slot)`, `host.position(slot)`, `host.yaw(slot)`, `host.presses(slot, "grab")`.
2. `demo/modes/show/items_ui/<id>_view.gd` extends `item_look.gd`: `draw_icon`, `begin_fx` / `update_fx` / `end_fx`, `mods`, `camera`, `begin_shot` / `update_shot` / `end_shot`, `banner_kind`. `flip_view.gd` and `boost_view.gd` are the shortest examples.
3. Add the id to `catalog.gd KINDS` (the order is the wire's), its defaults and tuning ranges to `items.json`, and the id to `tools/cast_check.py`.
4. `tools/modes/items/<id>_scenario.gd`: an authority and two replicas end to end (below).

## The first use (the hitch) and the dress rehearsal

The first time a machine draws an item's materials, particles, shaders, fonts and footage the renderer builds them on the spot. With a cold Metal shader cache (a fresh install or build, another Mac) the first SMASH, CALL, FORCED VIDEO, GLITCH, BOOST or SOAR of a run would freeze the game for 0.3 to 2.5 s; the second use of the same item costs one normal frame (measured with `tools/modes/items/hitch_scenario.gd`, below). With a warm cache the first uses cost 12 to 52 ms. The clock's own BOOST / SOAR (14 to 22 s into a race) is the same first use.

So the first race round's card plays every item once before anyone can use one: `modes/show/items_ui/items_rehearsal.gd`, started by `items_view.gd` on its first state with items (once per run, never headless). It runs the real look code on a second manager (`items_view.gd` with `rehearsal` on) that is fed a made-up state for 52 frames (every effect on two slots from start to end, the shots, the three banner kinds, a line, the pill, a box popping) on a scratch rig, inside a SubViewport of its own (own World3D, camera and sun; the window's anti-aliasing, scaling and environment, so the pipelines are the ones the round uses). It touches nothing of the round: no mods, no camera, no HUD slot, sounds at -80 dB. A new look needs nothing for it except `rehearsal()` when its effect reads `a` / `b` or has no `duration` tuning (`item_look.gd`).

Measure it: `COLD` runs need the Metal cache of the offscreen binary moved aside (`$TMPDIR/../C/<bundle id>/com.apple.metal`, inside the render lock): `lockf -k ~/.local/state/friends-slots/render.lock timeout -k 10 600 <ItemsLab offscreen> --summer-offscreen --audio-driver Dummy --fixed-fps 60 --resolution 1280x720 --path demo -s "$PWD/tools/modes/items_harness.gd" -- --scenario=hitch --show-level=31 --view=1 --order=smash,call,dance --rounds=2 --measure=/tmp/hitch.csv --cast=<cast.json>`, then `python3 tools/modes/items_hitch_report.py /tmp/hitch.csv` (worst frame per item and use; `--view=0` is the victim's screen; the scenario switches the clock's events off).

## Checks

* `tools/modes/items_harness.gd -- --scenario=<id> --show-level=31`: the authority (match controller and shell, three scripted racers) and two replica clients with the show's real view and HUD; a scenario hands an item out, presses ITEM, and checks the state, the victim's view, the effects the owners' machines get and the end. `FRIENDS_PRIORITY=1 tools/engine_slot.sh timeout -k 10 280 "$SUMMER" --headless --audio-driver Dummy --rendering-driver dummy --fixed-fps 60 --path demo -s "$PWD/tools/modes/items_harness.gd" -- --scenario=flip --show-level=31`.
